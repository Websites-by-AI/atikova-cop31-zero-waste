Grounded in the $100M XPRIZE Carbon Removal Benchmark

The project was entered into the prestigious XPRIZE Carbon Removal challenge supported by the Musk Foundation. The technical focus is tackling the highest-density, short-cycle carbon emissions: organic waste decomposition.

When food waste rots anaerobically in landfills, it generates methane (CH₄). By deploying software and logistics to divert commercial organic waste to bio-methane digesters, black soldier fly larvae conversion, and high-grade compost, Atikova transforms a costly liability into carbon credits and regenerative products.

Our Vision & Commitment

From grassroots perseverance in Istanbul to international climate diplomacy
