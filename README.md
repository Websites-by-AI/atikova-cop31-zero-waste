**Atikova** is a digital climate innovation platform developed to tackle commercial and urban organic waste, landfill methane emissions, and circular economy supply chains in Turkey and international markets leading up to **COP31**.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Focus

1. **45% Landfill Organic Diversion:** Stopping the largest single source of municipal landfill methane (CH₄) emissions.
2. **10 Actionable Sectors:** Connecting hospitality, retail supermarkets, cold chain logistics, food processors, composting plants, packaging bodies, and municipal authorities.
3. **AI-Powered Proposal Automation:** Generating data-driven ESG partnership pitches and cold outreach templates via Hugging Face & Cloudflare Workers AI.
4. **COP31 Road-to-Antalya Demonstration:** Establishing verifiable corporate pilots across Istanbul and Antalya to present concrete carbon reduction case studies.

## 📊 10 Strategic Sectors & Corporate Targets

| # | Sector | Key Targets | Value Proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Hospitality & HORECA** | Divan Group, Swissôtel, Hilton | Kitchen food prep waste reduction & Zero Waste Certification |
| 02 | **Supermarkets & Food Retail** | Migros Türk (Gıdaya Saygı), CarrefourSA, Metro | Dynamic perishable discounting & food bank routing |
| 03 | **Municipalities & Public Bodies** | İSTAÇ A.Ş. (İBB), Fatih & Kadıköy Belediyeleri | Street-market organic scrap intake & neighborhood composting |
| 04 | **Cold Chain Logistics** | Netlog Lojistik, Ekol Lojistik | Fleet spoilage early warning & reverse logistics |
| 05 | **Industrial Food Processing** | Yıldız Holding (Ülker), Sütaş, Anadolu Efes | Industrial organic by-product valorization (spent grain, whey) |
| 06 | **Composting & Agro-Inputs** | Buğday Derneği, OYAK / Hektaş | Urban bio-waste to high-grade soil humus & carbon sink |
| 07 | **Packaging & Materials** | ÇEVKO Vakfı, PAGEV | Separating organic waste to improve post-consumer plastic recycling |
| 08 | **CleanTech Accelerators** | Impact Hub Istanbul, İTÜ Çekirdek CleanTech | Corporate pilot matchmaking & incubation programs |
| 09 | **Universities & Research** | İTÜ ARI Teknokent, Boğaziçi İklimBU | Academic LCA and empirical methane abatement verification |
| 10 | **Global Bodies & COP31** | UNDP Turkey Accelerator Lab, TEMA, Zero Waste Foundation | Showcasing UN SDG 12.3 progress at the COP31 Climate Summit |
